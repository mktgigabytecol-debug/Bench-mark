In paid media and in-person presence, AORUS and GIGABYTE currently compete with less noise than almost every other brand in Colombia. But this benchmark found something more important than that silence: it isn’t that the brand has never played this game. Between 2018 and 2022, AORUS ran its own regional esports league — AORUS League — with a dedicated North Cone branch that included Colombia. That branch went dark; the South Cone branch is still running, active and covered by global esports outlets.

The read for the North Cone plan: there is no need to invent an esports and paid-media strategy from scratch. A brand-owned, proven playbook already exists, one that simply stopped being used in this region. The cheapest opportunity isn’t competing head-on against Acer’s or Lenovo’s paid-media volume — it’s reactivating mechanisms the brand already built.

Beyond the competition, this benchmark’s biggest finding is about AORUS itself: the brand had its own internationally-recognized esports program, with a North Cone branch that included Colombia, allowed to go dark since 2022 — while the South Cone branch of that same program keeps running, growing, and getting global esports coverage.

What Changed

Vs. the previous review
  • 01Samsung Odyssey stops being an empty space: it now runs the Odyssey Cup, a proprietary tournament with a São Paulo regional final, plus recent active paid media with discount coupons.
  • 02Lenovo Legion escalated its esports bet: it’s now the official hardware partner of the Esports World Cup 2026, already reflected in several simultaneous active campaigns on Meta.
  • 03ASUS ROG combines the largest official Meta paid-media volume with a strong Google Ads presence: alongside Samsung, it’s the most consistent brand across both paid channels.
  • 04LG UltraGear, which looked as low-profile as AORUS on organic social, turns out to have the strongest search paid-media investment of any brand compared, through a local Colombian entity.
  • 05In in-person activations, only HP Omen and MSI have confirmed, Colombia-specific presence; AORUS has the mechanism (Pringles) but hasn’t brought it to the country.
  • 06Colombia’s gaming market keeps expanding: 15.5% revenue growth in 2023, with a projected USD 356 million by 2028.
